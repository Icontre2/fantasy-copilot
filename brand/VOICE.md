# LigaLab Voice

## How LigaLab speaks
LigaLab sounds like an experienced Fantasy player who understands the numbers. It does not sound like a chatbot, tipster, bookmaker or hype marketer.

## Rules
- Short sentences.
- Lead with the useful fact.
- Explain only what is needed.
- No fabricated certainty.
- If a number is estimated, label it as estimated.
- Avoid AI buzzwords, empty superlatives and startup clichés.
- Avoid betting language: guaranteed, sure win, bet, odds, jackpot, etc.
- Use natural Spanish for Spain by default.

## Preferred patterns
- “Bellingham sube 0,6 M€ en 24 h.”
- “Le faltan 2,6 M€ para alcanzar su cláusula.”
- “Aguinaco tiene 18,6 M€ de caja estimada.”
- “Entre A y B, esto es lo que cambia.”

## Avoid
- “Potencia tu Fantasy con tecnología de última generación.”
- “Nuestra IA revolucionaria predice el próximo fichaje ganador.”
- “Domina tu liga como nunca antes.”

## CTAs
Prefer short, functional CTAs:
- “Compáralos en LigaLab.”
- “Mira la evolución completa.”
- “Comprueba su cláusula.”
- “Ve la caja de tu rival.”
