# LigaLab Content Rules

Every content agent MUST read `brand/BRAND.md` and `brand/VOICE.md` first.

## Hard constraints
1. Never invent player values, clauses, injuries, lineups, cash balances, percentages or performance metrics.
2. Never fabricate a LigaLab screen. Use a real screenshot or a clearly labelled conceptual marketing graphic.
3. Never modify the LigaLab product UI as part of a marketing task.
4. Do not use unlicensed club/player imagery in paid assets without review.
5. Do not publish automatically until the workflow explicitly marks an asset `approved`.
6. One content item = one core user problem.
7. The first 1–2 seconds of short video must communicate the problem or tension.
8. Brand visuals must remain replaceable: do not bake a provisional logo into templates in a way that cannot be swapped.

## Default content mix
- Decision/comparison.
- Market value movement.
- Clause risk/opportunity.
- Rival economy.
- Probable XI / selection doubt.
- Squad insight.
- Timely Fantasy-relevant football event.

## Scoring dimensions (0–100)
- User pain / relevance: 25
- Freshness / timeliness: 20
- LigaLab feature fit: 20
- Visual demonstrability: 15
- Conversation / sharing potential: 10
- Conversion potential: 10

Reject opportunities under 60 unless explicitly requested.
