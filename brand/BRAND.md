# LigaLab Brand Operating System

## Non-negotiable scope
- DO NOT MODIFY THE LIGALAB PRODUCT UI from these brand rules.
- The red/black identity applies to: logo, app icon, App Store / Google Play assets, social media, ads and marketing collateral.
- Product UI keeps its current visual system unless there is a separate explicit product-design task.
- Logo/app icon is provisional and must be swappable without changing the rest of the marketing system.

## Brand idea
LigaLab is the control layer for a Fantasy league: understand your squad, rivals, market, clauses and league economy before making a decision.

Positioning: a premium, data-first Fantasy companion for users who want clarity and competitive advantage.

Primary tagline: **Tu liga. Tu ventaja.**

## Personality
- Analytical, not cold.
- Competitive, not aggressive.
- Premium, not elitist.
- Direct and clear.
- Technological without looking like a generic AI product.
- Football-native without looking like a betting app.

## Visual direction for marketing
- Base: near-black / white.
- Accent: deep LigaLab red. Current working token: #E21B23. This is provisional and can be refined.
- Positive data remains green. Negative/risk can use red. Never recolor semantic data purely for brand consistency.
- Avoid glossy AI-brand-board aesthetics, excessive glow, 3D logos, generic shields/crowns/flasks, betting visual language and visual clutter.
- Real product screenshots are preferred over invented interfaces.

## Logo direction
Current preferred direction: a minimal geometric LL monogram with an ascending gesture.
This is NOT final. The asset system must allow later replacement without touching content workflows.

## Product screenshots
Marketing must show the actual LigaLab product whenever a feature is being demonstrated. Do not fabricate screens, statistics or UI states.

## Content principle
Start with a real Fantasy problem, not with a LigaLab feature.

Pattern:
1. Problem / tension.
2. Relevant real data.
3. LigaLab interaction.
4. Resolution.
5. Short CTA.

## Examples
Good:
- “¿Vendo ahora o seguirá subiendo?” → value history / trend.
- “¿Cuál de estos dos ficho?” → comparator.
- “¿Puede mi rival pagarme la cláusula?” → reconstructed cash + clause.
- “¿Será titular?” → probable XI / percentage.

Bad:
- “Nuestra revolucionaria IA cambiará tu Fantasy.”
- “Domina a tus rivales con el poder de los datos.”
- Generic football news with no connection to a real user problem or LigaLab feature.
