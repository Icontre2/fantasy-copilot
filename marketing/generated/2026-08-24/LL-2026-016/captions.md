# LL-2026-016 — Captions

## TikTok / Reels / Shorts
No caption is required for the product proof itself.

If a platform requires post copy, keep it descriptive and neutral:

> Una forma de estimar la caja de un rival a partir de la actividad de la liga.

Do not state that the figure is exact. Do not claim that LigaLab knows whether a rival can afford a specific player. Do not add promises of winning, automatic alerts or availability.
