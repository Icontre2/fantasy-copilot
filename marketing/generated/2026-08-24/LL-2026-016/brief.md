# LL-2026-016 — RIVAL / CAJA

## Objective
Show one concrete reason a Fantasy manager would want to inspect a rival before acting in the market: LigaLab reconstructs the rival's available cash from league activity.

## Visual concept
Real Fantasy market listing → real rival profile → reconstructed rival cash.

The piece should work visually without a long explanation. The product proof is the point; no slogan is required.

## Product truth
- Feature: Caja de rivales reconstruida.
- The value is an estimate reconstructed from 100 M€ initial cash, paginated operation history and points income.
- Keep `≈` visible wherever the reconstructed cash is shown.
- Do not describe the estimate as the rival's exact balance.
- Do not infer or claim that the rival can or cannot afford a specific player.
- Do not show push notifications or automatic alerts.

## Capture requirements
- Real production LigaLab UI only.
- 9:16 vertical.
- Record 5–8 seconds so a clean 2–4 second section can be selected.
- Start with a real market context, then open a rival and reveal the reconstructed cash.
- Keep player/manager names and amounts authentic to the live session.
- Crop or blur unrelated personal information before approval.
- Do not alter figures, names, labels or UI to make the shot more attractive.

## Edit direction
- Fast cut from market context to rival profile.
- Let the cash estimate be readable for a moment.
- No intro logo.
- No explanatory paragraph.
- LigaLab identification can appear after the product proof.

## Copy
No hook is required. If text is necessary for comprehension, use only the minimum neutral label. Do not add Gen-Z slang, emojis, a slogan, or a promise that LigaLab prevents a rival from buying a player.

## Approval gate
Keep status `draft` until the real capture is supplied and a human reviews the creative. Do not generate or publish automatically.
