- 9:16 vertical
- 1080x1920 target
- Fast but readable pacing

Abstract football stadium tunnel / floodlight atmosphere, shallow depth of field, subtle handheld push-in. Kinetic Spanish text area reserved for: «Tu Athletic tiene demasiadas dudas hoy.»

Transition into a clean dark frame with a large empty portrait-oriented safe area designed to receive a REAL LigaLab screenshot in post. Add only exterior motion framing and a thin red accent line. Do not draw anything inside the screenshot area.

**9.5–12.5 s**
Optional second clean capture placeholder for a REAL LigaLab Comparador screenshot. If unused, continue subtle stadium/tactical motion and typography.

**12.5–15 s**
Premium minimal close: dark background, restrained red line sweep, space for replaceable LigaLab logo/app icon and CTA text.

## Motion
